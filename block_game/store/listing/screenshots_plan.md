# Cascado: plan of store screenshots and feature graphic / план скриншотов и баннера (версия 0.17)

Status: DRAFT. Nothing is uploaded. Publishing only after Vladimir's approval.

## Technical requirements (Google Play) / требования
- Phone screenshots: 2 to 8; JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no transparency / без альфа-канала); each side 320–3840 px;
  long side not more than 2× the short side; up to 8 MB each.
- Our size: **1080 × 1920** (9:16), PNG RGB 24-bit. At least 4 screenshots of 1080 px+ are needed for game promotions.
- Feature graphic (баннер): **1024 × 500**, JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no transparency, up to 15 MB.
- App icon (значок): **512 × 512**, 32-bit PNG (with alpha allowed), up to 1 MB. Source: assets/icon/icon_512.png.
- Files: graphics/phone_en/01.png … 08.png, graphics/phone_ru/01.png … 08.png, graphics/feature_1024x500.png,
  graphics/icon_512.png.

## Layout of each screenshot / как устроен скриншот
- Top band ~300–340 px of 1920: dark violet (#2A1446 → #3B1C63 gradient), caption 2–5 words, bold, bright
  (warm gold #FFD45C or white with violet outline), 90–110 px font, readable on a thumbnail.
- Below: real game frame (scaled 1080×1920 frame cropped or scaled to fit under the band). No fake features.
- No "Tetris", brands, "#1 / best / free", ratings, prices, fake reviews.
- English set shows the English game interface, Russian set the Russian one (the game has EN/RU).
