# Cascado: plan of store screenshots and feature graphic / план скриншотов и баннера

Status: DRAFT. Nothing is uploaded.

## Technical requirements (Google Play)
- Phone screenshots: 2 to 8; JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no transparency); each side 320–3840 px; ratio up to 2:1.
- Our game is portrait only, so make all screenshots **1080 × 1920** (9:16).
- To be eligible for game promotions on Google Play, upload at least 4 screenshots at 1080 px or more.
- Layout of each screenshot: caption at the top (about 20% of the height, large readable font, studio colors),
  real game frame below. No fake features, no elements that are not in the game.
- No "Tetris", no other brands, no "#1 / best / free", no ranking claims, no fake reviews on images.
- Language: English set for the main listing, Russian set for the Russian listing (same frames, different captions).
  IMPORTANT: the game interface is Russian-only now. English screenshots must show an English interface,
  so English text in the game is needed before the English screenshots can be made honestly.

## Feature graphic (banner) 1024 × 500
- Format: JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no transparency.
- Idea: a wide view into the crystal cave. On the left, the owl and the axolotl look at a wall of colored blocks;
  on the right, glowing crystals break out of the stone with a bright cascade of falling blocks and sparkles.
  Logo "Cascado" in the upper left area.
- Keep the center clear-ish: Google may put a play button over the center if we add a promo video.
- Keep all important things (logo, heroes) at least ~60 px away from the edges; the banner can be cropped.
- Minimal text: only the logo. No slogans with promises, no prices, no "free", no ranking words.
- Colors: deep violet/blue cave background, warm glowing crystals (amethyst violet, emerald green, fire orange,
  ice blue) so the banner stands out in both light and dark Play Store themes.
