**Chosen: `Cascado: Crystal Block Puzzle`** (29 characters)

Why: the title is the strongest search signal on Google Play. "Block puzzle" is the phrase people actually type,
"Crystal" is our unique goal (freeing crystals), and "Cascade" is already in the brand name "Cascado".

## 3. Full description (max 4000; this text is about 1,750 characters)

Plain text for copy-paste into Play Console (Play Console accepts plain text; bullets are the "•" symbol):

```
Deep inside a crystal cave, glowing gems are sealed in stone, and only your blocks can set them free.

Cascado is a calm and clever falling-block puzzle. Clear lines to break the rock around buried crystals. When a line disappears, the blocks above it crumble down and can complete new lines on their own. That is a cascade! Plan one smart move and watch the chain grow: Cascade ×2, ×3, ×4...

Pick your companion for the journey: a little owl who lives in a cozy tree hollow, or a smiling axolotl from an underwater grotto. Win levels, collect crystal shards and make your friend look great.

What's inside:
• 50 handcrafted levels in 5 halls of the cave: Amethyst, Emerald, Fire, Ice and the Heart of the Mountain. After level 50, the Endless Depths keep going.
• A clear goal in every level: free all the crystals.
• Cascade combos: one cleared line can trigger a whole chain reaction.
• Earn 1 to 3 stars. The fewer pieces you use, the more stars you get.
• Unusual piece shapes made of 2 to 5 cells keep every board fresh.
• Crystal shards for every win, new stars and big combos.
• A wardrobe of 16 items: hats, glasses, a crown, a cape and new colors.
• A home for each friend with 10 decorations of its own. The home grows richer as you go deeper into the cave.
• A cave map to follow your path and replay levels for more stars.
• Cute companions that blink, breathe and react when you tap them.
• Relaxing music and sounds, which you can turn off at any time.

Play your way:
• No ads and no in-app purchases.
• Works fully offline: no internet and no account needed.
• Made for portrait play on your phone.

Take a breath, drop a block and start the cascade.
```
