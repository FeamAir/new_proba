# Cascado Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Game version: 0.17

Cascado (the "Game") for Android is published by OWLIGHT Games ("we", "us").
This policy explains what data the Game handles.

## Summary

The Game **does not collect, share or sell** any personal data.
The Game does not connect to the internet.

## Data stored on your device

To keep your progress, the Game saves a small file on your device containing:

- the highest unlocked level;
- the number of stars earned on completed levels;
- sound settings (music and sound effects on or off).

This file stays in the Game's private storage on your device. It is never sent
to us or to any third party. It contains no name, contact details, location,
device identifiers or advertising data.

To delete this data, uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings
(Apps → Cascado → Storage → Clear data).

## What the Game does not do

- no registration or sign-in;
- no Android permissions requested (camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, etc.);
- no advertising;
- no in-app purchases;
- no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services;
- no use of the device advertising ID.

## Google Play

You install the Game through Google Play. Google may process information about
your device and the installation under its own policies, which we do not control:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

The Game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If we add advertising, purchases, Google Play Games leaderboards or analytics,
we will update this policy beforehand to state what data is collected, why, and
which third-party services receive it. The updated version will be posted on this
page with a new effective date.
